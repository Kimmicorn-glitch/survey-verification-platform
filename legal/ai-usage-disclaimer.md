## 3. Limitations

### 3.1 Accuracy

AI analysis is probabilistic, not deterministic. The Platform's AI systems:
- Are designed to achieve high accuracy but are not infallible
- May produce false positives (flagging legitimate data as suspicious)
- May produce false negatives (missing actual instances of fraud or irregularities)
- May have reduced accuracy for edge cases, unusual data patterns, or novel fraud techniques

### 3.2 Bias

AI models may exhibit biases based on:
- Training data composition
- Historical patterns in the data
- Regional or demographic variations
- Evolving societal norms

The Company continuously works to identify and mitigate biases in its AI systems.

### 3.3 Context Limitations

AI analysis may not fully account for:
- Cultural nuances in survey responses
- Regional variations in communication styles
- Legitimate but unusual response patterns
- Context-specific factors not captured in training data

### 3.4 Evolving Threats

AI models are trained on known fraud patterns. New or sophisticated fraud techniques may evade detection until models are retrained.

## 6. Blockchain Data Disclaimer

### 6.1 Immutability

Data recorded on the blockchain is immutable and cannot be altered or deleted. This means:
- Errors in recorded data cannot be corrected on-chain
- Personal information recorded on-chain is permanently visible
- You consent to the permanent recording of data when using blockchain features

### 6.2 Verification Limitations

Blockchain records verify that data was recorded at a specific time but do not guarantee:
- The accuracy of the recorded data
- The identity of the submitter
- The legitimacy of the underlying survey
