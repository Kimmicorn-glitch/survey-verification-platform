**BackOffice Pro (Pty) Ltd**

**Effective Date:** 1 January 2025
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. OVERVIEW

1.1 BackOffice Pro (Pty) Ltd ("BackOffice Pro") is committed to customer satisfaction. This Refund Policy outlines the circumstances under which refunds may be issued for the Survey Verification Platform.

1.2 This policy applies to all subscription plans and is supplementary to the Payment Terms and Master Services Agreement.

---

## 2. FREE TRIAL

2.1 The fourteen (14) day free trial can be cancelled at any time without any charge.

2.2 If a customer provides payment details and does not cancel before the trial end date, the subscription converts to a paid plan. A full refund of the first payment is available if requested within seven (7) days of the initial charge.

---

## 3. SUBSCRIPTION REFUNDS

### 3.1 Monthly Subscriptions

3.1.1 Monthly subscription fees are non-refundable for the current billing period.

3.1.2 Customers may cancel at any time, and the subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing period.

3.1.3 No automatic renewal charge will be applied after cancellation.

### 3.2 Annual Subscriptions

3.2.1 Annual subscriptions may be refunded within thirty (30) days of the billing date, subject to an administrative fee of ten percent (10%) of the annual subscription amount.

3.2.2 After the thirty (30) day window, annual subscription fees are non-refundable.

3.2.3 A pro-rata refund may be issued at BackOffice Pro's sole discretion for exceptional circumstances, calculated from the date of the refund request to the end of the billing period.

### 3.3 Enterprise Agreements

3.3.1 Refund terms for Enterprise agreements are governed by the applicable Master Services Agreement and Order Form.

3.3.2 Enterprise refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis.

---

## 4. USAGE-BASED CHARGES

4.1 Fees for consumed services (API calls, NFT mints, AI verifications, overages) are non-refundable once the service has been delivered.

4.2 If a technical failure on BackOffice Pro's side prevents service delivery, a credit will be applied to the customer's account.

---

## 5. SERVICE CREDITS

5.1 Service credits may be issued for:
   - (a) Verified SLA breaches as per the Service Level Agreement;
   - (b) Extended unplanned downtime exceeding four (4) hours;
   - (c) Billing errors attributable to BackOffice Pro.

5.2 Service credits are applied as offsets against future invoices and are not redeemable for cash.

5.3 Service credits expire twelve (12) months from the date of issue.

---

## 6. REFUND PROCESS

### 6.1 How to Request a Refund

6.1.1 Submit a refund request in writing to billing@backofficepro.com including:
   - Account name and email
   - Invoice number(s)
   - Reason for the refund request
   - Preferred resolution (refund or credit)

### 6.2 Processing Time

6.2.1 Refund requests are acknowledged within two (2) business days.

6.2.2 Refund requests are evaluated and decided within ten (10) business days.

6.2.3 Approved refunds are processed within fifteen (15) business days of approval.

6.2.4 Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

### 6.3 Denial of Refund

6.3.1 If a refund request is denied, BackOffice Pro will provide written reasons for the denial.

6.3.2 The customer may appeal a denial within thirty (30) days by contacting support@backofficepro.com.

---

## 7. EXCEPTIONS

7.1 Refunds may be denied or limited in the following cases:
   - (a) Violation of the Acceptable Use Policy;
   - (b) Fraudulent activity on the account;
   - (c) Abuse of the refund process;
   - (d) Services already consumed and delivered;
   - (e) Promotional or discounted purchases, unless required by law.
