## 1. Introduction

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") sets forth the rules and guidelines governing the use of the Survey Verification Platform ("Platform") provided by BackOffice Pro (Pty) Ltd ("Company", "We", "Us", "Our"). By using the Platform, you agree to comply with this policy.

---

## 2. Permitted Use

### 2.1 Authorized Activities

The Platform is designed for:
- Creating and distributing surveys for legitimate research purposes
- Collecting and analyzing survey responses
- Verifying the authenticity and integrity of survey data
- Calculating and managing trust scores for participants
- Recording verification results on blockchain
- Generating reports and analytics

### 2.2 Eligible Users

The Platform may be used by:
- Market research professionals and organizations
- Academic researchers and institutions
- Government agencies conducting public surveys
- Businesses conducting internal or external research
- Non-profit organizations conducting surveys

---

## 3. Prohibited Activities

### 3.1 Illegal Activities

You shall not use the Platform to:
- Violate any applicable law, regulation, or ordinance
- Conduct fraud, identity theft, or financial scams
- Distribute malware, viruses, or other harmful code
- Engage in money laundering or terrorist financing
- Facilitate human trafficking or exploitation
- Violate intellectual property rights

### 3.2 Platform Abuse

You shall not:
- Attempt to gain unauthorized access to the Platform or its systems
- Interfere with or disrupt the Platform's infrastructure
- Circumvent security measures, access controls, or usage limits
- Use automated tools (bots, scrapers, crawlers) without authorization
- Reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble any part of the Platform
- Impersonate another user or misrepresent your identity
- Create multiple accounts to circumvent restrictions

### 3.3 Data Abuse

You shall not:
- Submit false, misleading, or fraudulent survey data
- Manipulate or tamper with survey responses
- Submit responses using automated systems or bots
- Harvest or scrape personal information from other users
- Distribute personal information without consent
- Use the Platform to send spam or unsolicited communications

### 3.4 Content Restrictions

You shall not upload, submit, or distribute content that:
- Is defamatory, libelous, or slanderous
- Is obscene, pornographic, or sexually explicit
- Promotes violence, hatred, or discrimination
- Infringes on intellectual property rights
- Contains confidential information without authorization
- Violates any applicable law or regulation

---

## 4. Survey Integrity

### 4.1 Honest Participation

All survey respondents must:
- Provide truthful and accurate responses
- Complete surveys honestly and without manipulation
- Participate only once per survey (unless multiple submissions are explicitly allowed)
- Not share survey access credentials with others
- Not use automated tools to complete surveys

### 4.2 Fair Survey Design

Survey creators must:
- Design surveys that collect data for legitimate purposes
- Provide clear instructions and accurate descriptions
- Respect respondents' time and privacy
- Obtain appropriate consent before collecting personal information
- Not design surveys intended to deceive or manipulate respondents

---

## 5. Trust Score Integrity

### 5.1 Score Manipulation

You shall not attempt to:
- Manipulate trust scores through artificial means
- Create fake accounts to inflate trust scores
- Collude with others to artificially boost scores
- Use multiple identities to circumvent scoring

### 5.2 Score Respect

You shall respect trust scores as indicators and not:
- Use scores to discriminate unlawfully
- Share scores without authorization
- Make consequential decisions based solely on scores
