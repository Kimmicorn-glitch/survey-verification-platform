# Responsible AI Policy

**BackOffice Pro (Pty) Ltd**
**Survey Verification Platform**

---

**Effective Date:** 1 January 2025
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

1.1 BackOffice Pro (Pty) Ltd ("BackOffice Pro") is committed to the responsible development, deployment, and use of artificial intelligence in the Survey Verification Platform.

1.2 This Responsible AI Policy establishes the principles, practices, and governance frameworks that guide BackOffice Pro's approach to AI.

---

## 2. AI PRINCIPLES

### 2.1 Fairness and Non-Discrimination

2.1.1 AI systems shall be designed and trained to avoid unfair discrimination against individuals or groups.

2.1.2 Algorithmic bias is actively monitored and mitigated through:
   - Diverse and representative training data
   - Regular bias audits
   - Fairness metrics across demographic groups
   - Human oversight of consequential decisions

### 2.2 Transparency

2.2.1 BackOffice Pro is committed to transparency about how AI is used in the Platform.

2.2.2 Users are informed when AI is used in:
   - Fraud detection scoring
   - Trust score calculations
   - Duplicate detection
   - Survey verification decisions

2.2.3 AI-generated decisions that significantly impact users are explainable.

### 2.3 Accountability

2.3.1 Clear accountability is maintained for AI systems:
   - The AI Governance team is responsible for AI system oversight
   - Human reviewers are available for AI decisions
   - Appeal mechanisms are provided for automated decisions

### 2.4 Privacy

2.4.1 AI systems respect user privacy:
   - Personal information is processed in accordance with POPIA and GDPR
   - Data minimization principles are applied
   - Federated learning is explored for privacy-preserving model training
   - No raw survey responses are used for AI training without explicit consent

### 2.5 Safety and Security

2.5.1 AI systems are designed with safety and security in mind:
   - Adversarial robustness testing
   - Input validation and sanitization
   - Monitoring for model drift and anomalies
   - Graceful degradation when AI services are unavailable

## 3. AI GOVERNANCE

### 3.1 Governance Structure

3.1.1 The AI Governance Board includes:
   - Chief Technology Officer (Chair)
   - AI Engineering Lead
   - Data Protection Officer
   - Legal Counsel
   - Customer Representative (Enterprise)

3.1.2 The Board meets quarterly to review AI system performance, incidents, and improvements.

### 3.2 Model Lifecycle Management

3.2.1 AI models follow a structured lifecycle:
   - Development and testing
   - Bias and fairness assessment
   - Approval by the AI Governance Board
   - Staged deployment (canary, then full)
   - Continuous monitoring
   - Periodic retraining

### 3.3 Model Documentation

3.3.1 All AI models are documented with:
   - Model purpose and scope
   - Training data characteristics
   - Performance metrics
   - Known limitations
   - Bias assessment results
   - Update history

---

## 4. AI FEATURES IN THE PLATFORM

### 4.1 Fraud Detection

4.1.1 The fraud detection system uses statistical analysis and pattern recognition to identify potentially fraudulent survey responses.

4.1.2 Fraud scores are used as advisory inputs, not as sole decision-makers for consequential actions.

4.1.3 Users can request a human review of any fraud determination.

### 4.2 Trust Score Calculation

4.2.1 Trust scores are calculated using multiple weighted factors including response patterns, verification history, and behavioral analysis.

4.2.2 Trust score algorithms are regularly audited for fairness and accuracy.

4.2.3 Users can view the factors contributing to their trust score.

### 4.3 Duplicate Detection

4.3.1 Duplicate detection uses fingerprinting and similarity analysis.

4.3.3 False positive rates are monitored and minimized.

### 4.4 NFT Approval

4.4.1 AI-assisted NFT approval uses confidence scoring to assess verification eligibility.

4.4.2 Final approval authority rests with human verifiers.

---

## 5. HUMAN OVERSIGHT

5.1 AI systems that make or recommend decisions affecting users include human oversight mechanisms.

5.2 Critical decisions (e.g., account restrictions, fraud flagging) require human review before enforcement.

5.3 Users can always opt for human review of automated decisions.

---

## 6. DATA GOVERNANCE FOR AI

6.1 Training data is:
   - Sourced ethically and legally
   - Anonymized where possible
   - Representative of the target population
   - Regularly assessed for quality and bias

6.2 Data retention for AI purposes follows the company's data retention policy.

6.3 Customers' survey data is never used to train AI models for other customers.

## 7. ENVIRONMENTAL RESPONSIBILITY

7.1 BackOffice Pro considers the environmental impact of AI compute:

7.1.1 Model efficiency is optimized to reduce energy consumption.

7.1.2 Cloud providers with strong renewable energy commitments are preferred.

7.1.3 Model training is scheduled during off-peak energy periods where possible.
