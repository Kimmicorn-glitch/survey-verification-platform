We process personal data under the following legal bases (Article 6):

| Legal Basis | Processing Activities |

## 4. Data Subject Rights Under GDPR

| Right | Article | Description | Response Time |
|-------|---------|-------------|---------------|
| Right of Access | Art. 15 | Obtain confirmation and copies of personal data | 1 month |
| Right to Rectification | Art. 16 | Have inaccurate data corrected | 1 month |
| Right to Erasure | Art. 17 | Have data deleted | 1 month |
| Right to Restrict | Art. 18 | Limit processing of data | 1 month |
| Right to Portability | Art. 20 | Receive data in structured format | 1 month |
| Right to Object | Art. 21 | Object to processing based on legitimate interest | 1 month |
| Right re: Automated Decisions | Art. 22 | Not be subject to solely automated decisions | 1 month |

### 4.1 Exercising Rights

To exercise your GDPR rights:
- **Email**: dpo@backofficepro.co.za
- **Subject Line**: GDPR Rights Request
- **Include**: Proof of identity and specific request
- **Response**: Within 30 days (extendable by 60 days for complex requests)

### 6.1 Transfer Mechanisms

Personal data may be transferred outside the EEA using:

| Mechanism | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) | EU-approved contractual terms |
| Adequacy Decisions | Transfers to countries with adequate protection |

### 6.2 Transfer Impact Assessments

We conduct Transfer Impact Assessments (TIAs) for all international transfers to ensure adequate protection.

---

## 7. Data Protection Impact Assessments

In compliance with Article 35, we conduct DPIAs for:
- Large-scale processing of personal data
- Systematic monitoring of public areas
- Processing of special category data
- Automated decision-making with legal effects
- New technologies that pose high risks

---

## 8. Data Breach Procedures

### 8.1 Supervisory Authority Notification (Article 33)

- **Notification deadline**: 72 hours from awareness
- **Authority**: Relevant EU supervisory authority
- **Contents**: Nature, categories, consequences, measures, contact

### 8.2 Data Subject Notification (Article 34)

- **Notification**: Without undue delay
- **When required**: When breach is likely to result in high risk
- **Contents**: Nature, consequences, measures, contact

## 9. Privacy by Design

In compliance with Article 25, we implement:

- Default privacy settings
- Anonymization and pseudonymization
