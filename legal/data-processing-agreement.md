## 1. Definitions

**"Data Protection Law"** means POPIA (South Africa), GDPR (EU), and any other applicable data protection legislation.

**"Data Subject"** means the identified or identifiable natural person to whom personal information relates.

**"Personal Information"** has the meaning assigned in POPIA and/or GDPR, as applicable.

**"Processing"** means any operation performed on personal information as defined in applicable Data Protection Law.

**"Processor"** means BackOffice Pro (Pty) Ltd when processing personal information on behalf of a Controller.

**"Controller"** means the customer or organization that determines the purposes and means of processing personal information.

**"Sub-Processor"** means a third party engaged by the Processor to process personal information on behalf of the Controller.

## 2. Scope and Purpose

### 2.1 Purpose

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") governs the processing of personal information by BackOffice Pro (Pty) Ltd ("Processor") on behalf of the Customer ("Controller") through the Survey Verification Platform.

### 2.2 Details of Processing

| Element | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Subject Matter | Survey data collection, verification, and analysis |
| Duration | Duration of the service agreement |
| Nature | Collection, storage, analysis, verification, reporting |
| Purpose | Survey verification and data integrity assurance |
| Data Subjects | Survey respondents, users, organization members |
| Personal Information | As described in the Privacy Policy |

## 3. Processor Obligations

### 3.1 Compliance

The Processor shall:
- Process personal information only on documented instructions from the Controller
- Ensure that persons authorized to process personal information are bound by confidentiality
- Implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures
- Assist the Controller in responding to data subject requests
- Assist the Controller in ensuring compliance with Data Protection Law
- Delete or return personal information at the Controller's choice upon termination
- Make available all information necessary to demonstrate compliance

### 3.2 Sub-Processing

The Processor shall not engage another processor without prior specific or general written authorization of the Controller. Where general authorization is given, the Processor shall inform the Controller of any intended changes and give the Controller the opportunity to object.

### 3.3 Data Transfers

The Processor shall not transfer personal information to a third country or international organization without appropriate safeguards, including:
- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
- Binding Corporate Rules (BCRs)
- Adequacy decisions
- Explicit consent of the Controller

---

## 4. Controller Obligations

The Controller shall:
- Ensure it has a lawful basis for processing personal information
- Provide documented processing instructions to the Processor
- Ensure data subjects have been informed of the processing
- Respond to data subject requests in a timely manner
- Conduct data protection impact assessments where required

---

## 5. Security Measures

### 5.1 Technical Measures

- AES-256 encryption at rest
- TLS 1.3 encryption in transit
- Multi-factor authentication
- Access controls and audit logging
- Regular security assessments
- Incident response procedures

### 5.2 Organizational Measures

- Staff training on data protection
- Confidentiality agreements
- Access controls and need-to-know
- Regular compliance audits
- Data protection impact assessments

---

## 6. Data Subject Rights

The Processor shall assist the Controller in responding to data subject rights requests, including:
- Right of access
- Right to rectification
- Right to erasure
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to data portability
- Right to object

---

## 7. Data Breach Notification

### 7.1 Notification to Controller

The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay (and within 48 hours) after becoming aware of a personal information breach.

### 7.2 Notification Contents

The notification shall include:
- Nature of the breach
- Categories and approximate number of data subjects affected
- Likely consequences of the breach
- Measures taken or proposed to address the breach
- Contact details for further information

---

## 8. Term and Termination

### 8.1 Term

This DPA shall remain in effect for the duration of the service agreement.

### 8.2 Termination

Upon termination, the Processor shall, at the Controller's choice:
- Return all personal information in a structured, commonly used format
- Delete all personal information within 30 days
- Certify deletion in writing
