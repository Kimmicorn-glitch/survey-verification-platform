# Code of Conduct

**BackOffice Pro (Pty) Ltd**
**Survey Verification Platform**

---

**Effective Date:** 1 January 2025
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

1.1 This Code of Conduct applies to all users of the Survey Verification Platform provided by BackOffice Pro (Pty) Ltd ("BackOffice Pro").

1.2 By accessing or using the Platform, you agree to abide by this Code of Conduct.

---

## 2. ACCEPTABLE USE

2.1 You agree to use the Platform only for lawful purposes and in accordance with this Code of Conduct.

2.2 You shall not use the Platform to:
   - (a) Violate any applicable local, national, or international law or regulation;
   - (b) Transmit or store material that is unlawful, harmful, threatening, abusive, harassing, tortious, defamatory, vulgar, obscene, or invasive of another's privacy;
   - (c) Impersonate any person or entity or misrepresent your affiliation;
   - (d) Interfere with or disrupt the Platform or servers or networks;
   - (e) Attempt to gain unauthorized access to any part of the Platform;
   - (f) Upload malicious code, viruses, or harmful content;
   - (g) Engage in any form of automated data collection without authorization;
   - (h) Conduct fraudulent, deceptive, or misleading surveys;
   - (i) Use the Platform to discriminate against protected groups;
   - (j) Violate intellectual property rights of others.

---

## 3. SURVEY INTEGRITY

3.1 Surveys conducted through the Platform must:
   - (a) Be honest, accurate, and not misleading;
   - (b) Comply with all applicable laws and regulations;
   - (c) Respect respondent privacy and consent;
   - (d) Not collect more data than necessary;
   - (e) Provide clear and honest descriptions of purpose.

3.2 The following are strictly prohibited:
   - (a) Submitting fraudulent survey responses;
   - (b) Incentivizing dishonest responses;
   - (c) Manipulating survey data or results;
   - (d) Using the Platform to conduct phishing surveys;
   - (e) Harvesting personal information without consent.

---

## 4. BLOCKCHAIN AND NFT USE

4.1 NFTs minted through the Platform represent verified survey certifications.

4.2 You shall not:
   - (a) Mint NFTs for surveys that have not been verified;
   - (b) Misrepresent the origin or verification status of an NFT;
   - (c) Use NFTs for money laundering or other financial crimes;
   - (d) Manipulate blockchain records or data.

---

## 5. USER INTERACTIONS

5.1 Users shall interact with other Platform users respectfully and professionally.

5.2 Harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, or discrimination of any kind is prohibited.

5.3 Commercial solicitation or spam is prohibited unless conducted through approved Platform features.

---

## 6. DATA AND PRIVACY

6.1 You must comply with all applicable data protection laws, including POPIA and GDPR.

6.2 You shall not:
   - (a) Access or use another user's data without authorization;
   - (b) Share login credentials with unauthorized parties;
   - (c) Attempt to circumvent data protection measures;
   - (d) Collect personal information from respondents without proper consent and legal basis.

---

## 7. API USE

7.1 API access must be used in accordance with the API documentation and terms.

7.2 You shall not:
   - (a) Exceed rate limits or attempt to bypass them;
   - (b) Share API keys with unauthorized parties;
   - (c) Use the API to build competing products without authorization;
   - (d) Cache or store API responses beyond permitted limits.

---

## 8. REPORTING VIOLATIONS

8.1 If you become aware of a violation of this Code of Conduct, please report it to:
   - **Email:** conduct@backofficepro.com
   - **Online Form:** backofficepro.com/report

8.2 Reports should include:
   - Description of the violation
   - Evidence (screenshots, URLs, etc.)
   - Your contact information (optional for anonymous reports)

---

## 9. ENFORCEMENT

9.1 BackOffice Pro reserves the right to take action against users who violate this Code of Conduct, including:
   - (a) Warning notices;
   - (b) Temporary suspension of access;
   - (c) Permanent account termination;
   - (d) Legal action where appropriate;
   - (e) Reporting to law enforcement authorities.

9.2 Actions are proportionate to the severity and nature of the violation.

9.3 Users will be notified of enforcement actions and may appeal within thirty (30) days.

---

## 10. AMENDMENTS

10.1 BackOffice Pro reserves the right to update this Code of Conduct at any time.

10.2 Material changes will be communicated via email or Platform notification at least thirty (30) days before taking effect.

10.3 Continued use of the Platform after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated Code of Conduct.
