## 3. Nature of Blockchain Data

### 3.1 Immutability

Blockchain technology provides an immutable ledger, meaning:
- Once data is recorded, it cannot be altered or deleted
- All transactions are permanently and publicly visible
- Data integrity is cryptographically guaranteed

### 3.2 Decentralization

Blockchain data is stored across a distributed network of nodes:
- No single entity controls the data
- Data availability depends on network health
- The Company cannot remove or modify data once recorded

### 3.3 Transparency

All blockchain transactions are publicly viewable:
- Transaction hashes, timestamps, and addresses are public
- Smart contract interactions are auditable
- Verification records can be independently verified

## 4. Data Recorded on Blockchain

### 4.1 What is Recorded

The Platform may record the following on the blockchain:
- Verification result hashes (not raw data)
- Trust score values
- NFT certificate metadata
- Timestamps and transaction references
- Participant addresses (pseudonymous)

### 4.2 What is NOT Recorded

The following data is NOT recorded on the blockchain:
- Raw survey responses
- Personal identifiable information in plaintext
- Complete survey data
- User passwords or credentials

### 4.3 Consent

By using blockchain features, you consent to the permanent recording of verification-related data on the applicable blockchain network. This consent cannot be withdrawn for data already recorded.

## 5. Risks

### 5.1 Technical Risks

- **Network Congestion**: High network usage may cause transaction delays or failures
- **Smart Contract Bugs**: Despite auditing, smart contracts may contain vulnerabilities
- **Fork Risks**: Blockchain forks may affect data consistency
- **Oracle Failures**: External data feeds may be delayed or unavailable
- **Private Key Loss**: Loss of private keys results in permanent loss of access

### 5.2 Financial Risks

- **Gas Fees**: All blockchain transactions incur gas fees in the native token (MATIC)
- **Price Volatility**: The value of native tokens may fluctuate
- **Fee Increases**: Gas fees may increase during network congestion
- **Unpredictable Costs**: Transaction costs cannot be precisely predicted

### 5.3 Regulatory Risks

- **Regulatory Uncertainty**: Blockchain regulations are evolving globally
- **Legal Status**: The legal status of blockchain records varies by jurisdiction
- **Tax Implications**: Blockchain transactions may have tax consequences
- **Compliance**: Ensuring compliance with local laws is your responsibility

- Can be transferred but not duplicated

### 6.2 Ownership

NFT ownership is determined by the blockchain address controlling the private key:
- The Company is not responsible for lost or stolen private keys
- Ownership transfers are irreversible

### 6.3 Value

NFT certificates:
- Have no guaranteed monetary value
- May not be accepted by all third parties
- Are not securities or financial instruments
