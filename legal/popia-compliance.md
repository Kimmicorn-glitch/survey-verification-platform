## 3. Conditions for Lawful Processing

### 3.1 Condition 1: Accountability (Section 8)

The Company ensures that all personal information processed through the Platform is done in compliance with POPIA. We are accountable for all personal information under our control.

### 3.2 Condition 2: Processing Limitation (Section 9-10)

We process personal information only:
- With the consent of the data subject, or
- Where necessary to perform a contract, or
- Where required by law, or
- Where necessary to protect a legitimate interest, or
- Where necessary for a public law duty

### 3.3 Condition 3: Purpose Specification (Section 11-13)

Personal information is collected for specific, explicitly defined, and lawful purposes and is not processed in a manner incompatible with those purposes.

### 3.4 Condition 4: Further Processing Limitation (Section 14)

Personal information is not further processed in a manner that is incompatible with the purpose for which it was collected.

### 3.5 Condition 5: Information Quality (Section 15)

We take reasonable steps to ensure that personal information is complete, accurate, not misleading, and updated where necessary.

### 3.6 Condition 6: Openness (Section 16-18)

We maintain and publish this compliance statement and our Privacy Policy, describing how we process personal information.

### 3.7 Condition 7: Security Safeguards (Section 19-22)

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against:
- Loss, damage, unauthorized destruction
- Unlawful access, unauthorized modification, disclosure

### 3.8 Condition 8: Data Subject Participation (Section 23-25)

We respect and facilitate data subject rights to:
- Access their personal information
- Request correction or deletion
- Object to processing
- Complain to the Information Regulator

## 5. Data Subject Rights Under POPIA

| Right | Section | Description |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| Right to Access | Section 23 | Request confirmation and copies of personal information |
| Right to Correction | Section 24 | Request correction or deletion of inaccurate information |
| Right to Delete | Section 24 | Request deletion of personal information |
| Right to Object | Section 11(3) | Object to processing on reasonable grounds |
| Right to Complain | Section 99 | Lodge complaint with the Information Regulator |

### 5.1 Exercising Your Rights

To exercise any right under POPIA:
1. Email: iro@backofficepro.co.za
2. Include proof of identity
3. Specify the right you wish to exercise
4. We will respond within 30 days as required by POPIA

## 7. Data Breach Procedures

### 7.1 Detection and Assessment

We maintain automated monitoring systems to detect potential data breaches.

### 7.2 Notification

In the event of a data breach:
- **To the Information Regulator**: Within 72 hours of awareness
- **To affected data subjects**: As soon as reasonably possible
- **Documentation**: All breaches are documented regardless of notification requirement

### 7.3 Content of Notification

Notifications include:
- Description of the breach
- Categories and number of data subjects affected
- Likely consequences
- Measures taken or proposed
- Contact details

---

## 8. Cross-Border Transfers

Personal information may be transferred outside South Africa only where:
- The recipient country has adequate data protection laws
- Appropriate safeguards are in place (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses)
- The data subject has given explicit consent
- Transfer is necessary for contract performance

---

## 9. Direct Marketing

In compliance with Section 69 of POPIA:
- We do not send unsolicited direct marketing without consent
- We provide clear opt-out mechanisms
- We respect data subject objections to direct marketing

---

## 10. Children's Personal Information

In compliance with Section 34 of POPIA:
- We do not process children's personal information without consent of a competent person
- We verify age where appropriate
- We provide additional safeguards for minors' data
