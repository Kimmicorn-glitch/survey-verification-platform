## 3. BILLING CYCLES

3.1 **Annual Billing.** Annual subscriptions are billed in advance on the anniversary of the subscription start date. A discount of fifteen percent (15%) applies to annual billing.

3.2 **Monthly Billing.** Monthly subscriptions are billed in advance on the first day of each calendar month.

3.3 **Usage-Based Billing.** Overage charges are billed in arrears on the first day of the month following the usage period.

3.4 **First Invoice.** The first invoice is issued on the subscription start date and includes a pro-rata amount for the remainder of the current billing period.

4.1 **Accepted Methods.** BackOffice Pro accepts:
   - (a) Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard);

4.4 **Currency.** All prices are in South African Rand (ZAR). International customers may pay in USD or EUR at the prevailing exchange rate on the invoice date.

5.4 **Collection Costs.** The Client is responsible for all costs of collection, including reasonable attorney fees and collection agency fees.

5.5 **Disputed Invoices.** The Client must notify BackOffice Pro of any disputed invoice within ten (10) business days of receipt. Undisputed portions remain payable according to standard terms.
